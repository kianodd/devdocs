February 5, 2025

Django 5.1.6 fixes several bugs in 5.1.5.

Bugfixes

  • Fixed a regression in Django 5.1.5 that caused validate_ipv6_address() and validate_ipv46_address() to crash when handling non-string values ([36098]).
  • Fixed a regression in Django 5.1 where password fields, despite being set to required=False, were still treated as required in forms derived from [~django.contrib.auth.forms.BaseUserCreationForm] ([36140]).
