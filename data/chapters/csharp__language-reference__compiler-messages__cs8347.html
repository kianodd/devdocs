Cannot use a result of 'expression' in this context because it may expose variables referenced by parameter 'refParam' outside of their declaration scope

This error indicates the risk of having the captured 'refParam' reference pointing to a dead allocation stack. This could happen when after executing the 'expression', the stack will be destroyed, but the referenced variable (previously allocated on that stack) will continue to be pointed at by what was passed as 'refParam'.
