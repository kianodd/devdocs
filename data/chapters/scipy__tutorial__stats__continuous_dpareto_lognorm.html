For [x > 0], real [mu], [sigma > 0], [alpha > 0], and [beta > 0], the PDF of a double Pareto lognormal distribution is:

begin{eqnarray*}

: f(x, mu, sigma, alpha, beta) = frac{alpha beta}{(alpha + beta) x} phileft( frac{log x - mu}{sigma} right) left( R(y_1) + R(y_2) right)

end{eqnarray*}

where [phi] and [Phi] are the standard normal PDF and CDF, respectively, [R(t) = frac{1 - Phi(t)}{phi(t)}] is a Mills' ratio, [y_1 = alpha sigma - frac{log x - mu}{sigma}], and [y_2 = beta sigma + frac{log x - mu}{sigma}]. The CDF is:

begin{eqnarray*}

: F(x, mu, sigma, alpha, beta) = Phi left(frac{log x - mu}{sigma} right) -phi left(frac{log x - mu}{sigma} right) left(frac{beta R(y_1) - alpha R(y_2)}{alpha + beta} right)

end{eqnarray*}

The raw moment of order [k], for $-\beta < k < \alpha$, is given by:

begin{eqnarray*}

:

mu_k' = frac{alpha beta}{(alpha - k)(beta + k)}

: exp left(k mu + frac{k^2 sigma^2}{2} right)

end{eqnarray*}
