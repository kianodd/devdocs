September 26, 2016

Django 1.8.15 fixes a security issue in 1.8.14.

CSRF protection bypass on a site with Google Analytics

An interaction between Google Analytics and Django's cookie parsing could allow an attacker to set arbitrary cookies leading to a bypass of CSRF protection.

The parser for request.COOKIES is simplified to better match the behavior of browsers and to mitigate this attack. request.COOKIES may now contain cookies that are invalid according to [6265] but are possible to set via document.cookie.
