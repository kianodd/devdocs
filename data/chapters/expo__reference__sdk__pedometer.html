Pedometer from expo-sensors uses the system hardware.Sensor on Android and Core Motion on iOS to get the user's step count, and also allows you to subscribe to pedometer updates.

warning Pedometer is not available in Expo Go for Android. Expo Go does not include the ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission that Pedometer needs. With this permission, Google Play could classify Expo Go as a health app. To use Pedometer on Android, create a development build.
