There are two shape parameters [c,d > 0] and the support is [x in [0,infty)]. The Burr12 distribution is also known as the Singh-Maddala distribution.

begin{eqnarray*} fleft(x;c,dright) & = & {cd} frac{x^{c-1}} {left(1+x^{c}right)^{d+1}} \ Fleft(x;c,dright) & = & 1 - left(1+x^{c}right)^{-d}\ Gleft(q;c,dright) & = & left((1-q)^{-1/d}-1right)^{-1/c}\ Sleft(x;c,dright) & = & left(1+x^{c}right)^{-d}\ mu & = & d , Bleft(d-frac{1}{c}, 1+frac{1}{c}right)\ mu_{n} & = & d , Bleft(d-frac{n}{c}, 1+frac{n}{c}right)\ [m](){d} & = & left(frac{c-1}{c d + 1}right)^{1/c} ,text{if }quad c>1 text{, otherwise }quad 0\ [m](){n} & = & left(2^{1/d}-1right)^{-1/c} end{eqnarray*}

where [B(a, b) = frac{Gamma(a)Gamma(b)}{Gamma(a+b)}] is the Beta function.

Implementation: [scipy.stats.burr12]
