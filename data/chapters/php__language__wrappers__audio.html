ogg:// (PECL)

This wrapper is not enabled by default

In order to use the ogg:// wrapper, the OGG/Vorbis extension available from must be installed.

  • ogg://soundfile.ogg

  • ogg:///path/to/soundfile.ogg

  • ogg://http://www.example.com/path/to/soundstream.ogg
