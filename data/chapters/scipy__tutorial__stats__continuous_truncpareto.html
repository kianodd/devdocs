Two shape parameters, exponent [b>0] and upper truncation [c>1]. The support is [1leq x leq c].

The standard form is

begin{eqnarray*} fleft(x;b, cright) & = & frac{b}{1 - c^{-b}}frac{1}{x^{b+1}}\ Fleft(x;b, cright) & = & frac{1 - x^{-b}}{1 - c^{-b}} \ Gleft(q;b, cright) & = & left(1 - left(1 - c^{-b}right)qright)^{-1/b}.end{eqnarray*}

The non-central moments are given by

mu_{n}^{prime} = begin{cases}displaystylefrac{blog c}{1 - c^{-b}} & mbox{if $n = b$,}\ displaystylefrac{b}{b-n}frac{c^b - c^n}{c^b - 1} & mbox{otherwise.}end{cases}

The entropy is

hleft(Xright)= -left[logleft(frac{b}{1 - c^{-b}}right) + (b+1)left(frac{log c}{c^b - 1} - frac{1}{b}right)right].
