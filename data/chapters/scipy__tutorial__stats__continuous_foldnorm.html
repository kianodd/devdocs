If [Z] is Normal with mean [L] and [sigma=S] , then [left|Zright|] is a folded normal with shape parameter [c=left|Lright|/S] , location parameter [0] and scale parameter [S] . This is a special case of the non-central chi distribution with one-degree of freedom and non-centrality parameter [c^{2}.] Note that [cgeq0] . The standard form of the folded normal is
