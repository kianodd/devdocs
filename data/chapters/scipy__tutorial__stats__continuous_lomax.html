This is Pareto of the first kind with [L=-1.0] . There is one shape parameter [c>0] and support [xgeq0].

begin{eqnarray*} fleft(x;cright) & = & frac{c}{left(1+xright)^{c+1}}\ Fleft(x;cright) & = & 1-frac{1}{left(1+xright)^{c}}\ Gleft(q;cright) & = & left(1-qright)^{-1/c}-1end{eqnarray*}

hleft[Xright]=frac{1}{c}+1-logleft(cright).
