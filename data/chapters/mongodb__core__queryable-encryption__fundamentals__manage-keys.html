In this guide, you can learn how to manage your encryption keys with a {+kms-long+} ({+kms-abbr+}) in your application.

Tip

Rotate and Rewrap

Rotating DEKs consists of rewrapping them with a new {+cmk-long+}. This guide uses the terms "rotate" and "rewrap" interchangeably.

Overview

This procedure shows you how to rotate encryption keys for {+qe+}.

After completing this guide, you can rotate your {+cmk-long+} ({+cmk-abbr+}) on your {+kms-long+}, and then rewrap existing DEKs in your {+key-vault-long+} with your new {+cmk-abbr+}.

:::warning As you rotate keys, confirm that they aren't used to encrypt any other keys or data before deleting them. Keep your previous {+cmk-abbr+} enabled on your {+kms-abbr+} until you rewrap every {+dek-abbr+} that uses it. If you delete a {+dek-abbr+}, all fields encrypted with that {+dek-abbr+} become permanently unreadable. If you delete a {+cmk-abbr+}, all fields encrypted with a {+dek-abbr+} using that {+cmk-abbr+} become permanently unreadable. ::

For tutorials detailing how to set up a {+qe+} enabled application with each of the supported {+kms-abbr+} providers, see Overview: Enable Queryable Encryption .

Procedure

Your DEKs themselves are left unchanged after rewrapping them with the new {+cmk-abbr+}. The key rotation process is seamless, and does not interrupt your application.
