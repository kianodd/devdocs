Prepr CMS is a headless CMS with built-in personalization.

Integrating with Astro

Prepr CMS provides a GraphQL API to connect your data to Astro.

Prerequisites

To get started, you will need the following:

  • A new or existing Astro project configured for on-demand rendering.
  • A free Prepr account
  • A Prepr environment with existing blog posts. These posts must include a title and content. Other fields are optional.
