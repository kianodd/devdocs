Learn the fundamentals

Once you are connected, the Fundamentals section teaches the everyday query patterns.

  • Reading data — Filter with where, project with select, sort, and paginate.

  • Writing data — Create, update, delete, upsert, and the bulk write variants.

  • Relations and joins — Read related records with include on PostgreSQL and MongoDB.

  • Transactions — Make several writes succeed or fail together.

  • Advanced queries — The SQL builder and the MongoDB pipeline builder for shapes the ORM can't express.
