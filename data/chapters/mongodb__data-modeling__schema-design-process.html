The schema design process helps you identify the data your application needs and organize it to optimize performance.

Use Cases

Plan and design your schema early in the development process. This helps prevent performance issues as your application grows.

MongoDB's flexible schema lets you evolve your data model iteratively. You can add new fields and new content types without rewriting existing documents or migrating data. Depending on your application, you may want to establish a simple schema to cover basic functionality before optimizing.

Tasks

The schema design process consists of the following steps:

Identify your workload

Identify the operations that your application runs most frequently. See data-modeling-identify-workload.

Map relationships

Identify the relationships in your application's data and decide whether to link or embed related data. See data-modeling-map-relationships.

Apply design patterns

Apply schema design patterns to optimize reads and writes. See data-modeling-apply-patterns.

Create indexes

Create indexes to support common query patterns. See create-indexes-to-support-queries.
