Nest is an MIT-licensed open source project whose ongoing development is made possible by the support of the community. The framework is the result of a long road of sleepless nights, work after hours, and busy weekends.

How can you help?

Nest isn't backed by a large company that continuously pays for the hours spent on its development. I rely entirely on the goodness ❤️ of the community. I would love to make the framework even better and stay fully focused on delivering solutions that make coding enjoyable. To make that possible, I run a few support platforms:

  • become a backer or sponsor on OpenCollective
  • send a one-time donation via PayPal
  • or reach me directly at mail@kamilmysliwiec.com

If you love Nest, or your business uses it, consider sponsoring its development to ensure that the project your product relies on is actively maintained and improved. Your support also helps me create more content for the whole Nest community, such as educational blog posts and videos.
