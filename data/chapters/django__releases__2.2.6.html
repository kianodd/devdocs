October 1, 2019

Django 2.2.6 fixes several bugs in 2.2.5.

Bugfixes

  • Fixed migrations crash on SQLite when altering a model containing partial indexes ([30754]).
  • Fixed a regression in Django 2.2.4 that caused a crash when filtering with a Subquery() annotation of a queryset containing django.contrib.postgres.fields.JSONField or [~django.contrib.postgres.fields.HStoreField] ([30769]).
