Payload limits and deadlines

Client-visible payloads are validated and measured before response headers are committed. Defaults are 2 MiB and 64 levels of nesting, and limits.mode is warn — a violation is logged with its property path and the payload is still served. Switch to enforce once the logs are quiet. deadlineMs (falling back to timeout) bounds lazy representation factories, context creation and projection, layout resolution, and rendering. These asynchronous hooks receive the same AbortSignal. A deadline that expires before the response is committed returns 503; one that expires mid-stream aborts the stream without injecting an error into the partial document.
