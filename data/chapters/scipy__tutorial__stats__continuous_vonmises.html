There is one shape parameter [kappa>0], with support [xinleft[-pi,piright]]. For values of $\kappa<100$ the PDF and CDF formulas below are used. Otherwise, a normal approximation with variance [1/kappa] is used. [Note that the PDF and CDF functions below are periodic with period [2pi].
