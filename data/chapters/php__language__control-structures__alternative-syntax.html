PHP offers an alternative syntax for some of its control structures; namely, if, while, for, foreach, switch, and declare. In each case, the basic form of the alternate syntax is to change the opening brace to a colon (:) and the closing brace to endif;, endwhile;, endfor;, endforeach;, endswitch;, or enddeclare;, respectively.
