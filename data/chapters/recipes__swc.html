SWC (Speedy Web Compiler) is an extensible Rust-based platform that can be used for both compilation and bundling. Using SWC with the Nest CLI is a straightforward way to significantly speed up your development process.

Hint

SWC is approximately 20 times faster than the default TypeScript compiler.
