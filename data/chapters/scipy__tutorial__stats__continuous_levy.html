A special case of Lévy-stable distributions with [alpha=frac{1}{2}] and [beta=1] and support [xgeq0]. In standard form it is defined for [x>0] as

begin{eqnarray*} fleft(xright) & = & frac{1}{xsqrt{2pi x}}expleft(-frac{1}{2x}right)\ Fleft(xright) & = & 2left[1-Phileft(frac{1}{sqrt{x}}right)right]\ Gleft(qright) & = & left[Phi^{-1}left(1-frac{q}{2}right)right]^{-2}.end{eqnarray*}

It has no finite moments.

Implementation: [scipy.stats.levy]
