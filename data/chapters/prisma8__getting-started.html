Prerequisites

  • Node.js 22.18 or newer on the 22 line, 24.11 or newer on the 24 line, or 26 or newer. Check with node --version. Node.js 24 is recommended. The Nuxt template needs 22.22.3 or newer on the 22 line, or 24.15 or newer on the 24 line, because Nuxt requires it.
  • A package manager. The npm that ships with your Node.js release works; on Node.js 22 that is npm 10. pnpm, Bun, and Yarn 4.10 or newer are also supported.

  • Add to PostgreSQL — Add Prisma ORM to an existing PostgreSQL app and infer a starter contract from the live schema.

After setup

  • Use the generated app scripts for the first run.
  • Open prisma-8.md or the installed Prisma ORM skills when you want agent-ready guidance inside the project.
  • Change the starter contract when you are ready to model your own data.
  • Open the Prisma ORM overview when you want the concepts behind the setup.
