The Poisson random variable counts the number of successes in [n] independent Bernoulli trials in the limit as [nrightarrowinfty] and [prightarrow0] where the probability of success in each trial is [p] and [np=lambdageq0] is a constant. It can be used to approximate the Binomial random variable or in its own right to count the number of events that occur in the interval [left[0,tright]] for a process satisfying certain "sparsity" constraints.
